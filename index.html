Spiritual Progression & Integration Map

"The Yoke and the Pipeline": A Mental Map of Integration

My reflective journey this block centered on a single image that kept returning to me during quiet meditation: the yoke. Drawing from Sister Alynda Kusch’s prompt about beasts of burden placed together in hoops of a yoke to share and carry a heavy load, I realized stepping into the Savior's yoke at baptism and endowment is not a restraint—it is a sharing of load ("I Tell You These Things That Ye May Understand," 2023). That realization forms the heart of my mental map, which intertwines who I am, what I believe, and how I engineer code.

My faith does not sit alongside my engineering like a separate app running in the background—it directly shapes specific technical decisions. When I write Terraform, I am practicing Purposeful Architecture (Moses 3:5), designing before provisioning. When debugging or maintaining 99.99% system uptime, I exercise Operational Diligence (D&C 64:33–34), recognizing that small, consistent tasks lay a great foundation. And as I monitor and handle databases, I operate in Consecrated Stewardship (Luke 16:10), with root-level access functioning as an ethical boundary.

Foundational Guiding Principles

1. Purposeful Architecture

Moses 3:5

Spiritual Creation Precedes Physical Creation: In enterprise DevOps, infrastructure is never provisioned haphazardly. Blueprinting architecture, security policies, and Terraform IaC definitions prior to deployment mirrors the divine pattern of intentional design.

Key Anchor: Upfront design prevents system downtime, technical debt, and security drift.

2. Operational Diligence

D&C 64:33–34

Great Things Proceed from Small Things: System stability and 99.99% platform uptime are built on small, disciplined daily habits—granular test suites, version-controlled commits, and proactive monitoring.

Key Anchor: Patient, incremental effort creates resilient cloud architectures and sustainable mastery.

3. Consecrated Stewardship

Luke 16:10 & 3 Nephi 13:22

Integrity in Reliability: Managing production access and payment pipelines is a sacred stewardship. Keeping my eye single to ethical service ensures user data privacy, platform security, and cross-functional transparency.

Key Anchor: Faithfulness in the smallest automation script honors both team and end-users.
